We’re excited to see how AI is transforming the way people write code. We encourage contributors to use AI tools to explore, learn, and enhance the Pyrefly codebase. We always expect a human in the loop who understands and can explain any content that AI generates on their behalf.

*Do not communicate via LLM-generated text.* Please do not rely on AI to speak for you in issues, PRs, or any other communication forum. Any LLM-generated text must be clearly marked and accompanied by substantial content you wrote yourself. For example: "Pyrefly reported this confusing error on my code: `<your own description>`. I asked Claude, and it thinks this is a bug in Pyrefly: `<LLM-generated analysis>`." Using AI for translation is acceptable as long as you include your original, untranslated text alongside the translation.

*Thoroughly review and understand any AI-generated code before submitting.* This practice helps us maintain high code quality standards, facilitates meaningful review discussions with maintainers, and increases the likelihood that your submission will be accepted. Please [split large PRs](CONTRIBUTING.md#splitting-a-pull-request) when possible.

*Contribute at human speed, not AI speed.* AI can be used to do many things very fast, but our project moves at human speed. Please do not do things like posting large amounts of LLM text back-to-back or rapidly claiming and generating solutions to a large number of issues.

*Do not use AI to one-shot good first issues.* Issues labeled as "good first issue" are intended to help newcomers learn about the codebase, and there are only a limited number of them available. Please do not use AI to complete these issues without any input of your own. By doing so, you miss out on learning the basics of Pyrefly and take the opportunity away from other new contributors. Using AI as a learning aid, especially for researching the codebase, is fully acceptable.

*Contributions from autonomous AI agents are not accepted.* We may close PRs that we believe were prepared without human involvement.
